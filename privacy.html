Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Who uses this app

This application is used solely by its owner to automate their own Gmail account through a personal n8n workflow. It is not available to the public.

What data is accessed

With the owner's authorization, the app accesses the owner's Gmail messages and labels in order to read, search and organize them.

How data is used

Data is used only to perform the owner's own automation tasks. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored securely within the owner's n8n instance. No Gmail data is stored outside the owner's own systems.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account settings under Security → Third-party apps with account access.
